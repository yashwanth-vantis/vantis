# Panright Studios: Brand Core

## 1. Tagline

1. **Know the film before the shoot.** (Recommended) The whole promise in a producer's own words: certainty first, spend second.
2. **Proof before principal photography.** Uses the industry's own term for the point where money becomes irreversible.
3. **Find the flaws before the audience.** Names the enemy: the opening-weekend verdict.
4. **Greenlight with evidence.** Speaks to the one decision a production head owns.
5. **Pan right. See the whole picture.** Ties the name to the camera move that reveals what the frame was hiding.

## 2. Brand idea

Panright moves a film's moment of truth from opening weekend to the day before the shoot.

## 3. Positioning statement

For production houses carrying a slate of scripts and a finite budget, Panright Studios is the filmmaking suite that tests each film on the page, then supports it through prep, shoot and edit. You commit money to a script that has already been questioned, and your crew keeps every creative decision.

## 4. Voice rules

| Rule | Say this | Not this |
|---|---|---|
| Speak in production terms | "Catch the second-act sag before the schedule locks." | "Optimise your content workflow." |
| One claim per sentence | "The script is tested. Then the money moves." | "Our comprehensive platform enables end-to-end production excellence." |
| The crew holds the credit | "Your editor makes the cut. Panright lines up the takes." | "Our AI edits your film." |
| State it, never sell it | "A weak third act costs less on paper." | "Game-changing script intelligence." |
| Name the money plainly | "Find the problem before a shoot day is spent." | "Mitigate downstream inefficiencies." |
| AI is a tool, mentioned once | "AI-assisted assembly. Final cut stays with the editor." | "Driven by cutting-edge AI." |

## 5. Ethics line

Panright does the reading, the checking and the paperwork, so the people who make films make every decision that matters.

Validation version: Better a hard note on the script than a hard verdict on opening night.

## 7. Boilerplate

**50 words**

Panright Studios is a filmmaking suite for production houses. It tests scripts and film ideas before money reaches the set, then supports the production through prep, shoot and edit. Named for the camera move, the pan right, Panright is built for filmmakers, not to replace them. The company is pre-launch.

**100 words**

Panright Studios is a filmmaking suite for production houses, independent filmmakers, studios and OTT teams. Its work begins before the shoot: scripts are shortlisted, ideas are tested, and weaknesses are found while they can still be fixed on the page. From there, Panright supports the production through preparation, the shoot and the edit, including AI-assisted assembly that leaves final cut with the editor. The company takes its name from the camera move, a pan to the right, and its position from one belief: a film should be validated before the shoot, not after the audience reacts. Panright Studios is pre-launch.
